अलर्ट से समाधान तक — एक नज़र में

A six-panel walkthrough of the operator's journey — from the WhatsApp alert to a completed, supervisor-notified diagnosis — including what happens when they get stuck.

1
The Alert

Filter Feed Pump में रिसाव — WhatsApp पर तुरंत अलर्ट आता है।

A leak is detected. The operator gets a WhatsApp message immediately, in Hindi, with a simple "reply 1 to start" prompt.

💬 WhatsApp
2
Into the App

1 लिखते ही एक लिंक आता है — टैप करते ही गाइडेड जाँच ऐप में खुलती है।

WhatsApp is just the doorbell. The real diagnostic flow — questions, checks, remedies — all happens inside the app via a single tap.

🔗 Deeplink → App
3
One Question at a Time

हर screen पर सिर्फ़ एक सवाल — बड़े, आसान बटन।

The flow is built on three UX principles: one decision per screen, minimal choices, and large thumb-reachable buttons — so the operator never feels overwhelmed mid-diagnosis.

📱 Guided Steps
4
Getting Stuck? Ask for Help

अटक जाएं? "मदद चाहिए" बटन दबाएं — categorised सवाल तुरंत जवाब देते हैं।

A persistent help button opens quick-question categories (about the step, tools, escalation, tech issues) plus a free-text option — so hesitation doesn't turn into a dropout.

🤖 Need Help
5
Diagnosis → Tool → Escalation

सही कारण चुना, सही टूल पहचाना, ज़रूरत पड़ने पर supervisor को सीधे सूचना गई।

Three decisions are captured in sequence: the likely root cause, the tool required to fix it, and whether the fault needed escalating — each auto-scored against the SME-staged answer.

🔧 Tool + 📤 Escalation
6
Every Metric, Captured Silently

Latency, dropout, root-cause accuracy, tool ID, escalation, help-seeking — सब अपने आप दर्ज होता है।

Nothing needs manual logging. Every tap timestamps itself into a per-operator record, ready for the researcher's Results view and Study Summary dashboard.
