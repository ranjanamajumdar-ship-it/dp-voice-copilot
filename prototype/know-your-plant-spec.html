🌿 Know Your Plant

An AI Plant Operations Copilot for wastewater treatment — a senior plant engineer standing beside the operator through every shift. Not a chatbot.

Surface Web + Mobile + Voice Users Plant operators, shift engineers Languages हिन्दी · English Environment Harsh industrial, gloves, glare Deliverable IA · Flows · Components · States
The product thesis

Operators don't need more graphs — they need a colleague who already read them. Know Your Plant reads live SCADA, history, SOPs and the plant's own biology, then explains, diagnoses, guides and predicts in plain language, always showing why and how confident it is.

01

Four experiences, one copilot

Know Your Plant is a single intelligence expressed through four modes. The operator never "chooses a mode" — the copilot shifts posture based on what the shift needs: understanding, learning, fixing, or doing.

Mode 01 · Understand
Know My Plant

An intelligent shift briefing in plain language. Answers "How is my plant today? What changed? What needs attention?" Prose over dashboards.

Proactive Surfaces before asked.

Mode 02 · Learn
Explain My Plant

Teaches using this plant — its equipment, live values, current mode and SOPs — not textbook generalities. "Explain today's DO trend."

Contextual Grounded in reality.

Mode 03 · Fix
Diagnose My Plant

Turns diagnostic trees into conversation. The AI asks the questions, narrows causes, and drives to root cause → tasks → verification → closure.

Guided Question-led, not flowchart.

Mode 04 · Do
Guide My Work

An interactive supervisor for procedures — sludge wasting, backwash, CIP, isolation. Live values, warnings, next step, verification, observations.

Supervised Watches while you work.

Design consequence: these are not four apps but four conversational postures over one shared context object (the current plant state). A single "Ask Your Plant" thread can flow Understand → Explain → Diagnose → Guide without the operator restating anything.

05

Know My Plant — the shift briefing

A narrated state-of-the-plant. It summarizes rather than plots, and always resolves to a short list of things worth the operator's attention.

Questions it answers
  • How is my plant performing today?
  • Is everything running normally?
  • What changed since my last shift?
  • Which process / equipment needs attention?
  • What's today's biggest compliance risk?
  • Will we meet discharge limits today?
Briefing anatomy
  • Verdict — one sentence, colour-coded
  • Health rollup — plant, then sub-scores
  • What changed — diff vs. last shift
  • Watch-items — ranked by risk × urgency
  • Recommended actions — max three, tap to act
  • Confidence note — how sure, and why
Interaction: every noun in the briefing is live. "Pump 2 vibration" opens the Equipment Expert; "compliance" opens the forecast; "clarifier" opens that process. The briefing is a narrated table of contents for the whole plant.

Diagnostic trees are re-authored as dialogue. The AI proposes ranked causes, the operator picks a path, and the copilot walks each check step-by-step — always ending in a closed loop.

Confirmed cause

↓

Isolate P3 → clean strainer → restore. Each links to Task Mode.

Rationale: a flowchart forces the operator to hold the map. Conversation lets the AI hold the map and the operator hold the wrench. The AI reads live values for each branch so the human only does the physical checks.

Biological Health Score

Not a measured parameter — an inferred AI score with a confidence level. It fuses many weak signals into one honest number, and always shows what it's unsure about.

Inputs fused
DOMLSSSVIpH ORPTempInfluent load Blower runtimeWasting history Lab reportsOperator observations

Returns

  • Biological Health 0–100
  • Confidence 0–100 (data completeness & agreement)
  • Explanation — which signals drove the score
  • Current risks + recommended actions
Readout
88

Stable biology. Good settling, DO on target.

Confidence 72% · no SVI in 3 days

Watch: slight foam increase reported this morning. If it thickens, check F:M ratio.

Honesty rule: when confidence is low, the score is shown muted with a "help me be sure" prompt (run a settling test, log an observation) rather than pretending certainty.

Data sources & AI reasoning contract

The copilot never answers from a single source. Every response is a synthesis, and the interface makes that synthesis visible — the operator can always see what the AI read.

Source palette

Live SCADAHistorical trendsIssue history Maintenance historyConfigurationProcess modes Equipment modesDiagnostic treesTask library SOPsOperator logbookShift handover Manual observationsLab reportsWeather API
Always show

Sources read — a "reading N sources" line the operator can expand to see exactly which.

Always show

Confidence — every inference carries a level and the reason it isn't higher.

Always show

Why — the evidence chain behind any recommendation, in one tap.

Reasoning rule: combine ≥2 sources before asserting. Live value + history gives "normal vs. abnormal"; add SOP for "what to do"; add issue history for "has this happened before." A claim backed by one source is shown as an observation, not a diagnosis.

System states — empty · loading · error · AI

In a control room, ambiguity is dangerous. Every state says plainly what's true and what to do — the copilot never spins silently or fails blankly.

Empty states

Where
Copilot says
No observations yet
"I don't know your biology's normal yet. Log one observation and I'll start learning your plant's fingerprint." → capture CTA.
No open issues
"Nothing needs fixing right now. Plant health is 91%." Reassurance, not a blank void.
New plant / cold start
"I'm still learning this plant. Scores will sharpen over the first two weeks." Confidence shown as low, honestly.

Loading states

Moment
Treatment
AI thinking
"Reading 4 sources…" with the sources naming themselves as they resolve — progress is informative, not a spinner.
Live value fetch
Skeleton in the sensor grid; last-known value shown greyed with timestamp until fresh.
Vision estimate
"Analysing settling photo…" with the four estimates streaming in as each completes.

Error & degraded states

Failure
Behaviour
SCADA offline
Banner: "Live data lost 07:04. Showing last-known values; diagnosis paused until reconnected." Never guesses on stale data.
Low confidence
Answer shown muted + "I'm not sure — here's why, and what would help me be sure."
Conflicting sources
"SCADA and the last lab report disagree on MLSS. Here are both — which do you trust?" Surfaces the conflict, doesn't hide it.
Out of scope
"That's beyond what I can see. Logging it for your shift engineer." Honest boundary, with a handoff.

14

Context-aware AI

The copilot always knows where the operator is. Pronouns resolve to the current screen, so "why?" is never met with "why what?"

On Pump 2

"Why?" → Why is Pump 2's vibration up?

On an alarm

"Is this serious?" → the copilot knows which alarm and answers with severity + history.

On a trend

"Explain this." → explains this DO curve, this window.

Context object: every screen exposes a small context payload (entity, live values, mode, time window) to the copilot. Follow-ups inherit it, so "Can this wait until tomorrow?", "Has this happened before?", "Show me similar failures" all resolve without the operator re-specifying. No repetitive prompts — ever.
15

Design principles

The rules that settle every trade-off, in priority order. When two conflict, the higher-numbered yields.

01
Worker firstDesigned for gloves, glare, noise and pressure — not for analysts at a desk.
02
Minimal cognitive loadOne verdict at a time; the AI holds the complexity.
03
Explain before alarmingContext arrives with the alert, never a naked red light.
04
Guide, don't instructWalk beside the operator; don't bark orders.
05
Teach while workingEvery action is a chance to build the operator's skill.
06
Predict, don't reactSurface tomorrow's risk today.
07
Show confidenceEvery inference wears its certainty openly.
08
Always explain whyNo recommendation without its evidence one tap away.
09
Recommendations over raw dataNumbers are available; conclusions lead.
10
Hindi & English, equalBilingual by default, including voice.
16

Future opportunities

Where the copilot grows once the foundation earns trust.

Fleet intelligence

Learn across plants (privately) to benchmark fingerprints and pre-warn of seasonal failure patterns.

Autonomous setpoints

Copilot proposes, then — with sign-off — trims blower/dosing setpoints inside safe bands.

Predictive maintenance

Vibration + runtime + history → "Pump 2 bearing likely fails in ~3 weeks; order the part."

Voice-only shifts

Full hands-free operation for field rounds, in Hindi or English.

Compliance autopilot

Draft regulatory reports from the logbook and lab data, operator approves.

Operator skill graph

Track what each operator has been taught; tailor depth and surface gaps to trainers.
